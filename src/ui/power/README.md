Owner area: screen 09, bidding power, and the PayPal window seen from the page that opens it. Ported from `mockups/09-web-bidding-power.html`, value for value, with the states of design/20 (frames 7 to 12).

- `PowerScreen`: the nav, the column and Glim's corner, from `getPowerView` (`src/server/holds/view.ts`).
- `PowerPanel`: the amount, the explainer and the Hold button. A paddle with a hold gets raise mode by itself.
- `HowTheHoldWorks`: the explainer. Every figure follows the amount chosen; the example win is 64% of it, as the approved $320 of $500.
- `HowTheRaiseWorks`: raise mode's two steps on the same Surface panel (design/20 frame 12), in place of the explainer.
- `useHoldWindow`: opens the window inside the click and follows it. News comes from the window (BroadcastChannel and postMessage, `src/app/paypal/window-message.ts`), the stream's `hold` rows, and polling `GET /api/holds/[slug]/[holdId]`, whichever arrives first. It refreshes the server's page when a hold lands, so the nav flips, and then goes back to bid.
- `hold-window.ts`: the popup decisions, with no React. The window opens on /paypal/start (design/17 frame 1). A blocked window, or one that closed before the server answered, turns the button into "Continue to PayPal in this tab" (design/17 frame 5), which marks this tab and sends it to PayPal.
- `hold-flow.ts`: the button's states as a reducer, and every state's words in one place: design/20's frames 9 to 11 (waiting, canceled, failed), and their nearest frame for still confirming, a lost window and a refused approval.

**Below 1440** (max-* variants only): laptops keep the 880 column with less room above it; below 1024 the column takes the width less 32px gutters (16 on a phone), and the 480 columns become full width up to 480. Glim's corner closes the page under the trust line (`CornerOrb placement="page-end"`), since at 1024x768 the resting "Not sure how much?" bubble sat on the Hold button with no way to scroll it clear. On a phone the title is 26, the amount chips stay four across at 48 tall, and the Hold button (with its notice, "Didn't open?" and the trust line) comes right after the amount, so it is on the first screen; How the hold works (or the raise's steps) follows it as reading, its three steps stacked (`order` on the explainers). Glim's "Not sure how much?" keeps only its question on a phone (`phone: "title"` in `PowerScreen`). The Other… field is 16px on a phone, so iOS doesn't zoom in on focus.

**Links to it:** `powerHref(links, next, raiseToCents)` in `power-href.ts`; 05's "Raise bidding power" passes the new total that covers the bid. `?next=` is where the guest goes after approving, checked by `backHrefFor`; `?amount=600` opens on an amount, and `?raise` (also `?raise=600`) says the same for links that mean a raise. An amount the rules refuse is ignored.
